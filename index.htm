    How to play

    Marigold color is controlled by one gene with four alleles: W, B, R and Y. Every plant carries two alleles. Your job is to work out how they interact.

    1. Plant starter seeds (white, blue, red, yellow). Water a seed within 15 seconds or it will probably fail.
    2. Sprouts need water and fertilizer within 15 seconds to bloom on time. Without both they may bloom late or wither.
    3. Blooming plants need water every 60 seconds. A thirsty plant (amber border) may wither.
    4. Like Mendel with his peas, you choose the crosses: with the Cross tool, pick two blooming plants that sit side by side. A seed lands on an empty tile nearby.
    5. Collect a bloom to sell it for points. You can also identify its genotype for a bonus, but a wrong call costs points.
    6. Fill in the field journal for every color.

    You finish when you have collected all 8 colors, completed all 8 journal entries, and correctly identified one blue plant of each of its genotypes. Your game saves automatically in this browser.
